✦Hope Valley Trust✦

Every child deserves a fair start in life.

Hope Valley Trust is a registered charitable trust working to bring quality education within reach of underprivileged children in Islamabad. Through Hope Valley School, the Trust has been opening doors since 2016 — helping girls and boys learn side by side, reaching families through their children, and building a community where every child can grow with dignity and hope.

    Girls and boys learning side by side.

  • Transforming Families

    Learning that reaches every home.

✦About Us✦

Welcome to Hope Valley School

Building Brighter Futures Through Education

At Hope Valley School, we believe that education is the foundation of a brighter future. We are committed to providing children with quality education, moral values, confidence, and a safe, caring environment where every child is encouraged to think, learn and succeed.

Registered under Charity Act 2021

30+ Years of Experience in teaching & school leadership

✦Meet Our Principal✦

Zermina Shakeel

Principal, Hope Valley School

With over 30 years of experience in teaching and school leadership, Zermina Shakeel leads Hope Valley School with a simple yet powerful belief:

Every child deserves to be seen, heard, and given a fair chance to shine.

Since 2016, under her guidance, Hope Valley School has been a nurturing environment where children learn with confidence, grow in kindness, and discover their potential.

✦Our Mission✦

Shaping tomorrow, one child at a time

Our Mission

To make quality education accessible to children and empower them with the knowledge, confidence, and values they need for a better future.

Our Vision

A future where every child has the opportunity to learn, dream, and achieve their full potential.

✦Why Hope Valley?✦

What makes our approach different

  • Why Us?

    Dedicated and experienced teachers, computer education, practical learning and educational trips, a library and character building classes — in a quiet, conducive educational environment.

  • Community Building

    Through meaningful education a child can overcome socioeconomic barriers. We want children within communities to receive our standard of education, so they can uplift their communities from within.

  • Education Technology

    Maximizing outreach and effectiveness through the use of technology. We believe technology is key in creating a truly sustainable and lasting impact in the education sector.

  • Cost Effectiveness

    Maximum impact per Rupee spent. Cost effective operations through a lean organizational structure, technology and laser focus on deliverables that give high value to our mission.
